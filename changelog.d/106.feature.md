`spec_gate.lint_proposal` now lints the shape authors actually file: the seven decision-grade SCR sections (presence + non-placeholder) plus `title`/`outcome`/`affected_repos`, with `artifacts` dropped (none exist at proposal time). `spec_gate.proposal_from_scr` is the single-home extractor that maps a real SCR body (+ its `.openspec.yaml`) to the lint input — title from the `## Subject:` line, outcome from `.openspec.yaml`, sections via the newly-public `scr_template.section_bodies`, affected_repos from the Routing section.
