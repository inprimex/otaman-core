Homed the bus-timestamp parser as `otaman_core.frontmatter.parse_bus_timestamp` (accepts the producer's fractional-second ISO-8601, a trailing `Z`, or an already-typed datetime; `None` for blank/unparseable) and routed `task_complete` through it. `validate_message` now rejects an empty or malformed `timestamp:` value instead of only checking the key is present — a timestamp that cannot be aged, archived, or ordered no longer validates clean.
