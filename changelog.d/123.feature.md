`verification-gates.yaml` hooks now carry a per-hook `target-role:`, the role `role-based` selects by — declared in config rather than supplied per dispatcher, so it is visible to the parse and resolvable from a bare checkout. A hook naming `role-based` with no `target-role` is refused at parse time (like a missing roles table), and `select_critics` resolves `ctx.target_role or` the hook's configured `target_role`, so a complete config resolves `role-based` with no caller instead of returning could-not-evaluate on every call.
