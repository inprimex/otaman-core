The merge lifecycle gate now requires **both** approval signals — `approved_by` present **and** `stage` at or past `spec-approved` — instead of `approved_by` alone. When the two disagree (one present, the other not) it refuses hard, naming both values, regardless of enforcement mode: two independently-written signals that can disagree are never silently reconciled. This catches both a mislabeled `approved_by` on a not-yet-approved change and a stage advanced without attestation.
