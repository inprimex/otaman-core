The merge lifecycle gate now accepts authoring merges: below `spec-approved` the honored field is `scr_approved_by` (authorization to author), and at/after `spec-approved` it is `approved_by` (the attestation). Previously the two-signal conjunct required `approved_by` at every stage, so it wrongly refused merges of authored-stage changes (which carry no `approved_by` by design). The two disagreement directions still hard-refuse regardless of enforcement mode.
