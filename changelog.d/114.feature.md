Added `otaman_core.registry_access` — the single access-contract chokepoint over the decision registers. `apply_transition` is the only status/field write path and always records an append-only transition (a transitionless status write is impossible by construction); authority actions (accept-cost/choose/reject-cost) require an approval record whose presence and shape core enforces. `load_register`/`save_register` are byte-equivalent (comment-preserving) via a new `ruamel.yaml` dependency, and `run_contract_suite` is the backend-agnostic contract test every backend runs.
