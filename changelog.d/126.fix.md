`parse_router_config` now refuses unknown keys in the `router:` block and in per-agent `route:` entries, instead of silently dropping them. A misspelled `local_only_classes` (e.g. `local-only`, the spelling `otaman policy routes` prints) previously parsed clean with an empty local-only set — leaving the sensitivity guard admitting everything while the tenant believed sensitive content was pinned local. The refusal names the stray key, matching `parse_verification_gates`' convention.
