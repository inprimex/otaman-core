`otaman_core.program_context` is the single-home program-context resolver: `enumerate_programs` lists programs over the `orgs/<org>/programs` layout with lifecycle state, `program_of_path` walks up from a cwd, and `resolve_program` applies the ruled precedence (explicit → cwd walk → TTY picker callback → non-interactive refusal). It never creates programs — the refusal lists the available programs and the `--program` form, advising `otaman init` only when none exist.
