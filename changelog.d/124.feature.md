`otaman_core.validate_message` now declares the message-type classification `MACHINE_EMITTED_TYPES` (types a verb or daemon owns and nobody hand-sends) and the derived `HAND_SENDABLE_TYPES` (everything else valid and unprivileged). This is the single home for the hand-sendable/machine distinction: `otaman send`'s allow-list was a second copy that drifted — `decision-required` fell out of it, and the never-block duty that requires emitting it before blocking became unperformable. Consumers import `HAND_SENDABLE_TYPES` instead of maintaining their own list, and a newly added type defaults to hand-sendable unless classified as machinery (fail toward sendable, never a silent unsendable).
