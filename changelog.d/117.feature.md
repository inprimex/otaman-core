Added the outcomes-registry schema and `registry_access.validate_register` (registry-access-contract 1.3). The schema pins the A.4 status enum (`Drafting|Backlog|Approved|In-Progress|Done|Retired` — `Done-Partial` abolished in favour of `Done` + a `remainder`) and is lenient elsewhere. `validate_register` reports violations as a list (never raises, not a load gate), with a migration hint for any remaining `Done-Partial` record, so the legacy rows stay readable until they are migrated.
