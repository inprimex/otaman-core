`task_complete.task_id_of` no longer truncates a letter-suffixed minor id (`2.4a` was read as `2`, colliding `2.3a`/`2.4a`/`2.4b` and never matching their filings); the suffix is now kept on every segment. `is_effectively_complete` gains `honor_all` and `release_gate.cut_eligibility` passes `honor_all=False`, so a blanket `--all` filing no longer makes a change with an individually-unfiled task cut-eligible. `cut_eligibility` also accepts a pre-read `filed=` map for one-pass fleet-wide evaluation.
