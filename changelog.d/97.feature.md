Added the critic-selection policy engine `otaman_core.verification_gates` and the `verification-gates.yaml` schema. Four named policies — `sensitivity-scoped`, `stakeholder-affected`, `consumer-chain`, `role-based` — are configured per hook (`primary`/`fallback` + `sensitivity-overrides`); `select_critics` resolves them and every result names the policy that fired. A clearance gate drops any critic lacking matching clearance when content carries a sensitivity class (recorded, never silent), and `clearances` declares which agents may review which classes.
