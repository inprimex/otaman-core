`otaman_core.review_policy` defines the Messages review policy — which message classes a human must see. `classify` sorts a message into mandatory / opt-in / auto-triage (urgent priority is always mandatory); `default_policy` is the shipped floor (decision points and approvals mandatory, task-complete and status broadcasts auto-triage, everything else opt-in); `parse_review_policy` applies a per-program override and names its source. A type declared both mandatory and auto-triage is refused.
